Hospital Management System for Emergency

A web-based emergency management application focused on quick access to hospitals and ambulance requests.

Features

  • Select a department and view nearby hospitals on a map.
  • Book appointments and request ambulances directly from the homepage.
  • Request specialized ambulances in critical situations.
